Problem

Money moves.
The record doesn’t.

The bank texts you

The debit notice arrives as a Telegram message. The app never sees it, so the expense has to be typed in by hand — and by evening nobody remembers what the 43 000 was for.

The bill splits four ways

One person pays for everyone, the rest work out their shares in a notes app or in their head. Nobody goes through the line items — it gets divided “roughly evenly”.

The debt lives in a chat

“I owe you for the taxi.” “You owe me for groceries.” Nobody nets the two, and a month later it is easier to forget than to bring it up.

A week later nobody remembers what they spent, or who owes whom.

Solution

The app knows
the expense —
and knows who
owes whom

One event — dinner for four — normally falls apart into four: an expense, a receipt, an argument about shares, and four debts. Here it stays one event from beginning to end.

The receipt becomes shares, the shares become debts, and the debts collapse into a single figure per person. Nobody does the arithmetic.

03/ 12Ouro Finance

Product · receipt

Photograph the receipt —
the items sort themselves out

Recognition pulls the lines, quantities, prices, VAT and service charge out of the photo. From there nobody types — they only correct whatever came out wrong.

  • Up to three frames of one long receipt merge into a single list
  • HEIC straight from an iPhone is accepted as is, no conversion
  • No receipt at all — items can be entered by hand, same steps
  • The draft saves itself: close the app and come back to it

On screen — a real receipt from the live demo: dinner at Chorsu Grill for 426 000 UZS. Screens are shown with the English interface, which is in localization now.

04/ 12Ouro Finance

Product · splitting

Items fly out
to the people

Evenly across everyone, item by item, or by splitting a single line between two. People come from contacts in one tap, and the “same as last time” preset repeats the arrangement from the previous dinner.

  • An “assigned 6 of 6” indicator refuses to save a half-split receipt
  • A friend paid — instead of your expense you get your debt to them for your share
  • The expense and its debts stay tied together: edit the total and the shares follow
  • The finished receipt sends as a link — Telegram unfurls it as a card
05/ 12Ouro Finance

Product · debts

Not a stack of IOUs,
one figure per person

Every debt with one person collapses into a net: opposing amounts cancel out, leaving a sum and a direction. The screen answers the only question people actually ask — who owes whom, and how much.

they owe you you owe them
  • Settle with a single payment: it closes that person’s debts oldest first
  • Partial payment, overpayment and forgiving the remainder each leave their own trace
  • The transfer fee books as a separate expense and never distorts the debt
  • Due date and privacy are set per debt

Integration · Telegram

Product · analytics

You can see where
the money goes

One page instead of tabs: income, expenses, balance and average spend at the top, then the category donut, spending pace with a forecast to the end of the period, and a comparison with the last one.

  • A separate “unreturned debts” segment — money that left but was not spent
  • The financial month starts where you want: paid on the 10th, month starts on the 10th
  • Swipe between periods, filter by account, day / month / year
  • Everything converts to the home currency — accounts can be in six
